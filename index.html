Our Story

Where Tradition
Meets Modern Elegance

At Gunugu, we believe jewellery is more than an accessory — it's a story. Over the years, we've been curating and crafting customized pieces that draw inspiration from India's rich regional craft traditions, reimagined in a modern, wearable aesthetic.

Located in the heart of Kukatpally, Hyderabad, our store brings together artisan-made pieces from skilled craftsmen across the country — each piece a reflection of heritage and contemporary design.

The Gunugu Promise

🔬

Hallmarked Gold

Every piece carries a BIS hallmark — 916 for our 22K collection, 750 for our 18K collection. Certified purity, trusted wear after wear.

🔬

925 Certified Purity

Every piece carries the 92.5% sterling silver mark. We guarantee purity you can trust, wear after wear.

✏️

Fully Customizable

Your vision, our craft. Personalize size, design, engraving, or stone settings — we make it uniquely yours.

Pan-India Artisans
